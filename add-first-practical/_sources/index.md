<br>**Where**: FPCE - UC Edifício 2, University of Coimbra, Portugal.

This is the website of the Popylar population receptive field (pRF) modelling workshop on November 5th and 6th 2026 at the University of Coimbra in Coimbra, Portugal. In this two-day workshop, we will present different applications of pRF modelling and how to implement them using the new prfmodel Python package. The workshop will contain a few lectures, but its main focus will lie on hands-on programming tutorials. We will use example datasets from different experiments (e.g., visual, tonotopic, numerosity) and neuroimaging domains (e.g., fMRI, ECoG).

If you are interested and want to attend, please sign up via this [link](https://ucstore.uc.pt/event/workshop-prf-2026-18/register) at the University of Coimbra homepage.

**Please note that there will only be a limited number of spots so that we can assist all participants during the hands-on sessions.**

## Practicalities
The workshop will be two full days (i.e., 9 am to 5 pm). Lunch will be provided and there will be  a social dinner on the 6th after the workshop. Both are included in the workshop fee of 125 Euros. Participants must organize and cover their own accommodation.

## Requirements
Participants should be either PhD students, postdocs or faculty. They should have basic programming skills in Python (e.g., they should know how to install packages, write a function, and work with Jupyter notebooks).

## What is the new prfmodel software?
prfmodel is a new Python package developed by the Spinoza Center and the Netherlands eScience Center. Its goal is to provide an easy-to-use yet flexible tool for fitting population receptive field models to neuroimaging data. prfmodel leverages GPU-accelerated backends (i.e., TensorFlow, PyTorch, and JAX) to fit models at break-neck speed and follows good research software development practices to ensure its sustainability.

You can take a peek at the development version of prfmodel on GitHub [here](https://github.com/popylar-org/prfmodel).
